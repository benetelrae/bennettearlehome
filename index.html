Civil Infrastructure Consultant

I help civil engineering firms and agencies assess their technology stack, implement Autodesk workflows that stick, and train teams to work smarter. Eight years in consulting, most recently at ARKANCE USA and before that Symetri USA.

Assessments & implementations

Technology assessments to find where a firm's workflow is losing time, then implementations that get Civil 3D and InfraWorks set up right the first time. Civil 3D and AutoCAD version rollouts, BIM and project execution plans, and Autodesk Construction Cloud and Forma workflows going back to BIM 360.

Standards, templates & DOT kits

Helped develop the MassDOT Civil 3D state kit and worked closely with other agencies such as the Pennsylvania Turnpike. Template and CAD standards work, plus automation that keeps drawings clean and consistent.

From pre-sales to on-site

Pre-sales presentations and proposal content, then delivery: on-site with a client supervising designers and drafters alongside the project manager, live technical support when things go sideways, and mentoring new consultants.
